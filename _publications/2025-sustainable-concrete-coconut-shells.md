### Abstract
The increasing demand for concrete in urban development raises significant environmental concerns associated with the extraction of natural aggregates. This study addresses the need for sustainable alternatives by evaluating coconut shells as a substitute for coarse aggregates in concrete. The primary objectives were to assess the impact of varying percentages of coconut shells (5%, 10%, and 15%) on the workability and strength of concrete, as well as to investigate the effects of superplasticizers on these properties.
