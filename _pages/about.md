I am a doctoral researcher in the Geo-Innovation Research Laboratory (GIRL) within the Department of Civil Engineering at Chonnam National University, South Korea, under the supervision of Professor Changho Lee. I also completed my Master of Science degree in Civil Engineering at Chonnam National University.

My research lies at the intersection of geotechnical engineering, advanced data-driven modeling, and signal processing. I focus on developing robust computational frameworks for geohazard risk assessment, unsaturated slope reliability, and non-destructive testing.

Research Interests

Geotechnical Reliability & Risk Assessment: Developing probabilistic frameworks using Machine Learning (ML) techniques for rainfall-induced landslide Factor of Safety prediction.

Physics-Informed & Bayesian Machine Learning: Building physics-anchored neural networks and uncertainty-quantified spatial models for geotechnical site characterization and settlement analysis.

Non-Destructive Testing & Signal Processing: Investigating acoustic signal processing, wave propagation techniques, and impact-echo simulations for material evaluation and structural health monitoring.

Unsaturated Soil Mechanics: Formulating limit-equilibrium models incorporating matric suction loss, transient groundwater dynamics, and Fredlund-Xing soil-water characteristic curve (SWCC) parameterizations.
